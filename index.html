Staff — sorters, dispatchers, receiving clerks & supervisors

Everyone who signs in to the Sorter app, each with their own 4–6 digit PIN. A supervisor PIN clears wrong-job alarms and reopens closed bags. Changes need a supervisor PIN (not needed for the very first supervisor).

Bag labels

Generic, pre-numbered bag labels (BAG-000123). Any sorter can use any label: the first bundle scanned in locks the bag to that job and dyelot. Numbers are never reused.
